# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- The randomizer now swaps legendaries too: Lugia's chamber, Mew's pier and
  the other legendary spots hold another strong Pokémon at the legendary's
  level, and legendaries can turn up anywhere a strong Pokémon would. (The
  roaming Raikou, Entei, Latias and Latios stay themselves.)
- Pokéwalker course names in the Day-Care man's Walks menu no longer run
  off their buttons.

## 0.1.0

The first public release: the open world, scaling Gyms, start-town and
starter choice, wandering wild Pokémon, every species catchable without
trading, the HM Kit, Day-Care Walks, the Lottery's Pokémon of the day, the
tuner, the optional randomizer and hard mode, and faster text, battles,
fishing and healing.
