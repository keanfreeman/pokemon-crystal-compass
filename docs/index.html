Pokémon Crystal Compass

Version 0.1.1 · About the hack

1. Choose your HeartGold ROM

An unmodified Pokémon HeartGold (USA) ROM, dumped from your own cartridge. It stays on your computer: patching happens in this page.

2. Patch it

You'll get Pokemon Crystal Compass v0.1.1.nds. Play it in melonDS or on a DS, and start a new game.

Prefer a desktop tool? Download the .bps patch from the releases and apply it with Flips. The clean ROM's SHA-1 is 4fcded0e2713dc03929845de631d0932ea2b5a37.
